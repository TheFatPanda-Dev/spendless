# Welcome (public landing)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: the owner on the way to Log in (most visits, often on the phone) and people sent the link while sign-ups are closed. Action: Log in; Create an account only when registration is open, otherwise a plain "Sign-ups are closed for now." Proof: the product's own interface with example figures, labelled. No testimonials, numbers or claims beyond PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract
FORM: seed: none (pinned). Owner, 2026-11-19: "Kit campaign look (Recommended): The identity kit's own campaign style from link-preview.png: black/mint split with the paper-ribbon image" and "Short product page (Recommended)".

THESIS: The kit's campaign poster, live. "Money made simple" on black beside the paper-ribbon photograph; refuses the logo-as-headline hero and gradient blobs.

OWN-WORLD: Kit black #080808 panel, Roboto Light display in snow, mint #09c98c wordmark and one mint filled action, hairline rule; paper-curls photo full-bleed. Below: sage canvas, mint hero card, ranked category bar, hairlines, no shadows.

STORY: Know what it is in one line; see the promise split in two — "See what went out" (category bar) and "Know what's left" (left-to-spend card); learn the plumbing facts (EU + UK banks, investments, € and £); log in.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desk: left half black with lockup + Log in top, headline at ~6rem Light, hairline, promise, actions; right half the photo, edge to edge. Phone: black block with headline and Log in above the fold, photo band below.

MEMORABLE: The poster made real, then the actual product cards under it; the close says something new, not the promise again.

MOTION: One staged fade-up of the headline block on load; reduced-motion shows it static.
